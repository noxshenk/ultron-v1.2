01

THE FACE

SSD1306 OLED behind a glass bezel, drawn at 60 fps from a living 1024×768 canvas. Blink rate, gaze and mood are all firmware — rewire the personality however you like.

02

THE VOICE

MAX98357A over I²S into a 36 mm 8-ohm driver. 3.2 watts — polite in a library, frankly loud in a bedroom. The grill is perforated steel, seated flush.

03

THE BRAIN

ESP32-S3 — dual-core Xtensa LX7 at 240 MHz with Wi-Fi and BLE 5.0 onboard. Dual USB-C ports for power and flashing. Arduino, ESP-IDF or CircuitPython — your call.
